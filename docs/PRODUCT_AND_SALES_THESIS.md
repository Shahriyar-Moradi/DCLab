# Product and B2B Sales Thesis

## The problem

Companies often have data-science/ML teams, but teams are expensive and bottlenecked by:
- data access
- data cleaning
- feature engineering
- hypothesis iteration
- experiment setup
- validation
- deployment
- monitoring
- cross-functional interpretation

The product should not attack the existence of internal ML teams.

## Positioning

Decision.ai is an acceleration and intelligence layer for internal data teams and business decision makers.

The pitch:

“Your team should not spend four weeks manually testing dozens of feature/model hypotheses for every business question. Decision.ai automates the experimental surface, preserves governance, and produces a traceable intelligence state that can feed recommendations and outcome simulations.”

## Initial buyer

Potential buyers:
- Head of Data Science
- VP/Director of Data
- Head of AI/ML
- Chief Data Officer
- Revenue/Marketing/Customer analytics leaders when the initial use case is business-specific

## Initial wedge

Do not sell the whole Decision.ai vision first.

Sell one measurable use case:
- churn reduction
- conversion prediction
- campaign optimization
- next-best-action
- pricing/discount optimization

## Proof of value

Run Decision.ai alongside the customer's existing process.

Compare:
- time
- model quality
- model breadth
- business uplift
- cost

If Decision.ai loses on predictive quality, investigate why.
If it wins, quantify the improvement.

## Enterprise trust

Required:
- data isolation
- lineage
- reproducibility
- audit trail
- model governance
- human approval
- uncertainty
- rollback
- monitoring

## Strongest claim

“Decision.ai turns a weeks-long exploratory ML workflow into an automated, measurable intelligence pipeline.”

Do not claim:
- universally better accuracy
- guaranteed causal simulation
- guaranteed replacement of data scientists
- more models always equals better results

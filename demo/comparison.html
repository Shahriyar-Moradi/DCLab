Previous plan, AI-first, and the adopted hybrid plan

All three share the finished Phases 0–3: a correct engine, the state graph and the agent interface (MCP). They differ in when agents arrive, how much they decide, and what keeps them honest. Cost and performance figures are estimates from stated assumptions, to be replaced by R1–R3 measurements.

Cost

Hybrid costs about 9× the previous plan in LLM spend, and 40% less than AI-first

The rule side of each cross-check costs nothing; the savings against AI-first come from a Sonnet lead agent by default, Batch API for Critic reviews and Jev for small judgments. Training compute is the same few tens of cents per project in all three.

Assumptions and limits

Read the numbers with these in mind

  • Week 0 is 2 October 2026 with Phases 0–3 merged (PR #18). Phase lengths are roadmap-style estimates; the AI-first and hybrid lengths are my estimates.
  • Token sizes and calls per project are assumptions; real use may be 0.5×–3×.
  • Prices are Anthropic list prices cached 25 Sep 2026 (Opus 5.5 $4 / $20, Sonnet 5.5 $2 / $10 per 1M tokens, cache reads $0.20). NOOA can route to other providers through LiteLLM. Jev's input price is from AGENTS_NOOA_JEV.md; its output pricing is not documented there and is treated as negligible.
  • Compute assumes CPU training at about $0.04 per vCPU-hour.
  • Accuracy, iteration and user-time effects are hypotheses for R1–R3 and design partners, not results.

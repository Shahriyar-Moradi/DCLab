Real precision and recall, by week outcomes arrive about 4 weeks late

Whether a customer really cancelled is only known about 4 weeks after the prediction. So the real precision you see today describes predictions from a month ago, and the drift chart is the early warning.

What the system does on its own and where it stops to ask you

  1. Detect driftDone by a rule every week: a column’s drift passes 0.20.automatic
  2. Explain itThe assistant writes a plain summary of what moved. Built, switched off.assistant
  3. Retrain on new dataUses the newer file and the same test design. You can undo it.automatic, you can undo
  4. Ask you before switchingThe retrained model is never used for scoring until you approve it.ask first

Thresholds change them and the rules follow

What changed

After the March marketing campaign, a new plan type, family_5g, appeared. It is now 6% of customers and churns about twice as often as the average. The model barely saw it in training, so it flags the wrong people: recall holds at 0.80, but precision fell from 0.63 to 0.48.

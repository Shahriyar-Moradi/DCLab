Goal & test design

What we want to predict, what “good” means, and how we will test it fairly.

What we predict

churned_next_30d

Yes or no: will this customer cancel in the next 30 days? A yes/no (classification) task.

How we rank models

PR-AUC

Only about 1 in 5 customers cancel, and PR-AUC rewards finding them without flagging many others.

Business rule

Recall ≥ 0.80

Catch at least 80% of the customers who will cancel. Anything below that is not acceptable.

How we test the same design is used for every run

{{chart:cv_timeline}}
Train: Jan 2025 – Mar 202639,306 rows · 5 time-ordered cross-validation folds Final test set: Apr – Jun 20268,904 rows · the last 3 months · used once, at the end

Why time-ordered? The churn rate rises over time, so each fold learns from the past and is checked on the months right after it. Shuffled rows would let the model peek at the future.

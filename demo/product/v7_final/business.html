How the model was built

It learned from 18 months of customer history and was checked on the last 3 months, which it had never seen. It looks at things like days since last login, recent fee changes and when the contract ends. It was tested once at the end, and not changed after.

Trust and limits

  • It ranks customers; it does not give exact chances.
  • A new plan type, family_5g, is not well covered yet. A retrained model is waiting for the data team’s approval.
  • Real results are known about 4 weeks after each list.

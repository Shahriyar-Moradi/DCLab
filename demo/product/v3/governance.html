Assistants in this workspace

Each one has a single job and can be switched off. With all of them off, DCLab still works with rules and forms.

Lab assistantRuns a project from your goal: prepares data, starts experiments, reports back.
Data investigatorExplains what the data checks found and asks you questions.
PlannerSuggests the goal, the test split and which models to try.
ReviewerReviews every finished run and points to the numbers behind each remark.
ImproverSuggests the next change to try in the improve loop.
Operations assistantWatches for drift, retrains and proposes new releases.
Quick checksFast yes/no judgments about columns, such as "is this an ID?".

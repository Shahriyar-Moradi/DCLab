PRODUCT BOUNDARIES

What this preview is—and is not

MVP direction

Structured-data ML; deterministic training and evaluation; project lineage; API, SDK/CLI, MCP; gated AI proposals; transparent evidence; human decisions; batch-first model operations.

After MVP / demand-gated

Online serving, persistent Jupyter, external GPU marketplaces, dual-cloud parity, general-purpose connectors, automatic business actions, and unrestricted code-running agents.

Review the gaps

Each product screen has “Review notes.” The product map collects roadmap coverage, open questions, and features deliberately left out.

Safe demo mode

All records are invented. Buttons open a preview and may mark an action “simulated” in this browser. No backend, model, compute worker, token, customer file, or external service is contacted.

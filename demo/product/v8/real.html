What’s real today

Every screen of DCLab with its status, written from what the product does today. Nothing is hidden: what is missing is named.

The whole project flow works today: data, goal and test design, experiments with 15 trust checks, improve (one change yourself, or a loop with sealed tries), the model and approving it for scoring, predictions for one file or every week, monitoring with retraining on newest data and editable thresholds, and history, plus the business view, usage and the platform console. The assistant and AI settings are a labelled beta that is not shown yet. Notifications and integrations work on the server but have no screen yet.

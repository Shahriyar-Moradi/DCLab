“8 of 10” and “6 of 10” come from the model’s one final test, on customers it had never seen.

How the model was built

It learned from 15 months of customer history and was then tested once on the latest customers, which it had never seen. It looks at things like days since last login, recent fee changes and when the contract ends. Nothing was changed after that test.

Trust and limits

  • It ranks customers; it does not give exact chances.
  • Reasons on the list say what the model used, not why a customer leaves.
  • A new plan type, family_5g, is not well covered yet.
  • Real results are known about 4 weeks after each list.

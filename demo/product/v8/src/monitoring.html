Retrain policy for this project owners and administrators

Off
DCLab never retrains on its own. A person can still retrain on a newer labelled file.
Ask me
When the newest week of the model in use is an alert and a newer labelled file was uploaded by a person who can start runs, DCLab asks in the Inbox: “Retrain on newest data?”. Nothing starts until a person does.
Automatic
In that case DCLab starts the retrain by itself, acting as the person who chose Automatic. It never switches the model in use.

The rule acts at most once per project in any 7 days, and never twice on the same file or week. Set to Ask me by Sam 3 weeks ago; Off is the default.

Thresholds owners and administrators can change them

Set by Sam, version 2, two weeks ago (precision floor off → 0.55, “Retention team capacity”). Weeks computed with new scored data after you save use the new values; past weeks keep the values they used. Nothing is computed again now. Platform staff and access tokens cannot change these.

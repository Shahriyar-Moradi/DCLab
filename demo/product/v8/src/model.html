The weekly scoring uses the model in use, and only if it has an approval. Today: Churn model v1, approved by you 12 weeks ago. Only scoring with the model in use is checked against its approval; other models can still score a file by hand.

Each approval or going back is one confirm step with your reason, and it adds a History entry. Nothing retrains here. Switching to the retrained v5 also moves the data, test design and features, so it is decided in the Inbox or on Monitoring.

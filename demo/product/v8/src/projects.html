Starting a project

New project is a three-step dialog: a name and a file; what to predict and the rule for success; then the test design the rules suggest, stated in words. Training starts only after you confirm, and your confirmation is saved as “Suggested by the rules · Confirmed by you”. The longer four-step page is still linked from the dialog. Everything is a form; no AI is needed.
